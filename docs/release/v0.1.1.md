Corrected release of **KAPSAM**, open-source software for the evaluation of measurement uncertainty (TÜBİTAK UME).
This is the version described in the revised article submitted to *Measurement*; every number of the article and
every file of its supplementary material is generated with it (`cargo test`, scripts in `tools/research/`). Changes
since v0.1.0 (research log D110–D112):

**Engine.** `--seed` and `--trials` now reach the consensus bootstrap and the multicomponent risks (they changed
only model and multivariate evaluations before). The public function `consensus::q_gamma_approximation` (gamma approximation of Cochran's Q) used a
linear term in the variance of Q that is not the variance of Q; it now uses the exact variance 2 tr[(AΣ)²], checked
by a new unit test against the matrix expression. The consensus evaluations were not affected: the bootstrap of the
NIST Consensus Builder uses its own function, which already had the exact variance. Validation reports label
consensus and multicomponent conformity projects by their procedure.

**Library (284 projects, 1841 reference values).** The guard-band projects (test uncertainty ratio) take their
reference values from the published formulas and the JCGM 106 risk integrals instead of Suncal. The two projects of an
unpublished laboratory procedure moved to `laboratory/` (they remain usable with `kapsam check --lab laboratory`).
The Monte Carlo tolerances follow one rule for every value — the larger of the documented tolerance and half a unit
plus five standard deviations over eleven seeds, independent of the program's distance from the reference — and were
tested with twenty further seeds (0 of 5300 comparisons outside); the Monte Carlo standard deviations and
correlations of the two examples with t₃ inputs (JCGM 102 9.4, TN 1900 E13), two seed-dependent GUM validation
outcomes and the Monte Carlo results of GUM-5 Example 14 (compared with its GUM estimate) are no longer reference
values. The report numbers of
the BIPM qNMR reports ISRD-06 and ISRD-07 are corrected (Rapport BIPM-2019/03 and 2019/04).

**Research.** Findings F101 and F102 (ISO 5725-3:2023 Table J.2 against the 1994 data), the reclassification of F40, F79,
F87 and F90, the recomputation record of the c1 findings and the check of errata and amendments
(`docs/research/recomputation-record.md`, `docs/research/errata-check.md`) and the map of the article's statements
to the code (`docs/research/claims-to-code.md`).
