# Monte Carlo propagation through the calibration fit

Generated by `python3 tools/research/benchmarks/calibration_mc.py` (research log D97). For every calibration project of the library the calibration data are drawn 1 000 000 times from normal distributions with their standard uncertainties and covariances, and the ISO 6143 fit is repeated for every draw (Lira, Measurement 44 (2011) 2156). The standard deviations of the parameters are compared with KAPSAM's linear propagation (exact derivatives of the solution, full Hessian; ISO 6143 A.2, A.6, A.7) and with the Gauss–Newton approximation (Suncal's York and ODR covariances); the numerical refit column checks the program's derivatives by central differences of the refit (A.2). The standard error of the Monte Carlo value comes from 20 batches.
