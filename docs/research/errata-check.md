# Errata and amendments of the documents with class-c findings

Checked on 2026-11-11 (research log D112), before submission, for every document with a settled c1 finding and for
the other documents of class-c findings that the article discusses. "None found" means: no erratum, corrigendum,
amendment or later edition listed by the issuing body on the date of the check; it does not mean that none exists in
a form the issuing body does not list (a national adoption, a committee draft, a private communication).
